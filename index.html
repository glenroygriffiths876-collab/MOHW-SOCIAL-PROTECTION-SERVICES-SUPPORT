Chapter Two

Referral Procedures.

Six steps for approaching referrals with compassion, privacy, and respect—drawn from the supplied guide.

♡
Empathetic practice reminder

People may feel ashamed, overwhelmed, or uncertain when asking for help. Use non-judgemental language, protect confidentiality, avoid promising eligibility, and explain that support services exist to help people through difficult periods.

!
Urgency and safeguarding

For immediate danger, serious medical distress, suspected abuse, suicidal thoughts, severe neglect, or child-protection concerns, follow facility emergency and safeguarding procedures first.
